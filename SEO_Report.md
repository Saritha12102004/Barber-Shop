# SEO Report — The Blade & Fade Barbershop Website

## 1. Website Titles
Each page has a unique, keyword-rich `<title>` tag combining the page purpose, business name and location, e.g. `Services & Prices | The Blade & Fade Barbershop, Karur`.

## 2. Meta Descriptions
Every page carries a unique `<meta name="description">` summarising that page's content in under 160 characters, written to encourage click-through from search results.

## 3. Meta Keywords
Each page includes a `<meta name="keywords">` tag targeting relevant local search terms (see keyword list below).

## 4. Heading Tags (H1–H3)
- One `<h1>` per page stating the page's core topic.
- `<h2>` used for major section headings (e.g. "Cuts & Fades", "Meet the Team").
- `<h3>` used for card/service-level headings (e.g. individual price list items, team member names).

## 5. Friendly URL Structure
All pages use short, descriptive, lowercase filenames with no query strings or IDs: `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `gallery.html`, `blog.html`, `contact.html`.

## 6. Alt Text for Images
All visual elements (gallery tiles, icons, the map embed) include descriptive `alt`, `aria-label`, or `title` attributes describing their content for accessibility and image-search indexing.

## 7. Internal Links
- Blog articles link to the Services and Contact pages.
- Home page links to Services and Contact.
- Footer navigation links to all major pages from every page on the site.

## 8. External Links
One authoritative outbound link from the blog (to the Wikipedia entry on barbering) is included with `rel="noopener"` and opens in a new tab, supporting content credibility without leaking link equity insecurely.

## 9. Keywords Used
`barber shop`, `men's haircut`, `fade haircut`, `beard trim`, `hot towel shave`, `barber Karur`, `men's grooming`

These terms are distributed naturally across page titles, meta tags, headings, and body copy — not stuffed.

## 10. Content
Total original body copy across the site (About, Services descriptions, Blog articles, Home sections) exceeds 700 words, written specifically for this business rather than generic filler text.

## 11. Mobile & Technical SEO
- Fully responsive layout (CSS Grid/Flexbox with breakpoints) — mobile-friendliness is a Google ranking factor.
- Fast-loading: no external image downloads; only two Google Fonts requests and one Maps embed.
- Semantic HTML5 elements (`header`, `nav`, `main` sections, `footer`, `article`) used throughout for better crawlability.
