About Us

Built by stylists, for regulars

Our Story

The Blade & Fade Unisex Salon opened on Trichy Road, Karur, with one goal: bring proper barbering and hairdressing craft — the kind you'd find in a big-city salon — to a neighbourhood shop, for men and women alike. No conveyor-belt haircuts, no rushed five-minute trims.

Our founder trained under senior barbers for years before opening these doors, and we later added a dedicated women's styling and colour team led by trained hairdressers. Every stylist who joins goes through a proper apprenticeship — on the men's side that's clipper work, scissor-over-comb, straight razor lines and beard shaping; on the women's side that's cutting, blow-dry technique and colour theory — before they're allowed to take a solo client.

Today the salon runs chairs for both men's grooming and women's styling, a dedicated beard and shave station, and a waiting area that regulars treat like a second living room. We're proud that most of our new clients come from word of mouth, not advertising.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Certified, apprenticeship-trained barbers and hairdressers
  • Fresh, sanitised tools for every client
  • Hot towel finish on every fade; conditioning finish on every colour service
  • Transparent, fixed pricing — no surprise add-ons
  • Appointment slots that are actually kept on time
  • Separate, comfortable stations for men's grooming and women's styling

Our Mission

Every client leaves looking sharper than they walked in

We measure a good haircut by one thing: whether you'd walk straight into a meeting, a wedding, or a first date right after leaving the chair. That standard doesn't change whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth.
